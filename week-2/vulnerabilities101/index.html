5. TryHackMe: Vulnerabilities 101

Practical exploration of vulnerabilities, then reflection for Northbank.

Purpose

This directed-study activity links theory to practice. You will explore how vulnerabilities are discovered and scored in industry tools, then relate one finding to Northbank.

Instructions

  1. Open the TryHackMe room: Vulnerabilities 101 on TryHackMe (opens in a new tab).
  2. Work through the tasks with your tutor’s guidance. You need a TryHackMe account.
  3. Complete both reflection fields below. Your work auto-saves in this browser.
  4. Mark complete and submit your formative result when both reflections are filled in.

Industry context: Severity scoring (for example CVSS) helps organisations prioritise patches. Higher scores indicate greater potential impact — but context matters for Northbank patient data and critical services.

OCR focus: Name the vulnerability clearly, explain how a threat could exploit it, and link to confidentiality, integrity or availability where relevant.

Submit your result

Submit after both reflections are complete. Detailed text stays in this browser; a summary is sent with your submission.
