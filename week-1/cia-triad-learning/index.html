Before you begin

Cyber security protects digital systems and the information stored, processed or transferred by them. This activity teaches the CIA triad and then checks your understanding with Northbank Community Health Partnership examples.

Instructions

  • Complete your details before starting.
  • Work through the learning stages in order.
  • Guided examples are not scored.
  • Check each scored section before moving on.
  • Answers lock after a section is checked.
  • Your result is not a formal grade.

Learn the CIA triad

Cyber security is not only about keeping information secret. Confidentiality is only one of the three aims. The CIA triad also includes integrity and availability.
