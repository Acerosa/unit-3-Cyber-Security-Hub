Week 6: Ethical, Legal and Operational Considerations

LO2 - Understand the issues surrounding cyber security. OCR specification section 2.6 Other considerations of cyber security.

Weekly learning outcomes

  • Explain ethical considerations that arise in cyber security practice, including responsible disclosure, employee monitoring and the boundary of ethical hacking.
  • Identify the main legal requirements placed on organisations and explain why legislation must be kept current.
  • Explain operational implications of security measures, including cost, staff time, system downtime, usability and lost productivity.
  • Analyse the implications of cyber security decisions for a range of stakeholders.

Ethics asks what ought to be done. Law asks what may, must or must not be done. Operational considerations examine whether a measure is practical to implement and use.

Examination focus

  • Name the statute and the relevant duty or offence together.
  • Answer legal questions with law rather than moral opinion.
  • Distinguish ethical, legal and operational considerations.
  • Structure a balanced OCR-style Discuss response that acknowledges a competing consideration before a justified conclusion.
  • A one-sided response limits available marks even where the writing is otherwise detailed.

Assessment checkpoints (formative)

  • LO2 diagnostic results and two revision priorities
  • Legislation matching activity
  • Stakeholder debate participation or reflection
  • Exercise in a Box decision record
  • OCR-style timed response and answer improvement
  • Completed LO2 revision organiser

These checkpoints are formative. This hub does not calculate an overall course grade.

Session summaries

  • Session 1: LO2 diagnostic, ethical and legal learning, operational trade-offs, government initiatives, then the tutor-facilitated NCSC insider threat exercise companion.
  • Session 2: Legislation retrieval, Northbank employee monitoring scenario, stakeholder debate, Discuss response skills and OCR-style practice.

Practical platform used this week

NCSC Exercise in a Box - tutor-facilitated classroom exercise: Insider threat resulting in a data breach at Northbank Community Health Partnership. The hub provides companion guidance and a decision record only; staged exercise prompts stay on the official NCSC materials.
