Sign in or register

Use the email and password for your learner account.

Creating an account registers you with Supabase Auth. A tutor still needs to link your account to a learner profile and group before submissions will work.

After you sign in

  • Open a Week 2–5 activity with ?backend=supabase to use the Supabase submit path while Apps Script remains the site default.
  • If your status shows “profile not linked”, ask your tutor to link your Auth user to a learner record and enrolment.
